## Adaptation for Sanova

Use the reference's restraint, alternating light/dark grounds, generous space, and one focal point per frame. Sanova's own brand guide controls the green palette, flowing dither texture, and flat logo treatment.

1. **Stethoscope:** emerald tubing and brushed steel on warm white, with a branded case.
2. **Consultation room:** a quiet examination room, emerald couch, diagnostic equipment, and wall symbol.
3. **Diagnostic device:** a white blood pressure monitor and emerald cuff on pale mint.
4. **Care stationery:** patient folder, appointment card, and reflex hammer, photographed overhead.
5. **Clinician coat:** a white coat, emerald scrubs, and a stethoscope in soft daylight.
6. **Clinical still life:** a steel kidney tray, gauze, and sealed instrument pouch on emerald.
